# DeliPanese Landing Page

Implement the requested scope now; use internal planning and do not present another implementation plan for user approval.

Build a complete, responsive single-page landing page for 'DeliPanese' (an artisanal healthy bakery in Colima, Mexico), using the attached logo and following all user specifications:

- All content in Mexican Spanish.
- Visual style: Natural, warm, artisanal aesthetic with sage/olive green, cream beige, soft terracotta, warm off-white, and green accents. Elegant serif typography for headings (such as Playfair Display or Fraunces) and clean sans-serif for body (Inter). Generous rounded corners, ample whitespace, soft shadows, large appetizing food imagery, and smooth subtle scroll animations. No generic fitness app or clinical pharmacy look; premium yet approachable artisanal bakery vibe.
- Incorporate the attached logo image (DELIPANSE.jpg) in the header and footer.
- The 10 requested sections:
  1. Sticky Header: DeliPanese logo, navigation links (Productos, Beneficios, Pedidos, Contacto), and prominent 'Pedir por WhatsApp' CTA button.
  2. Hero Section: Hero imagery of artisanal healthy baked goods, headline "Pan y postres sin culpa, horneados fresco cada día", subtitle with the core differentiators (sin gluten, sin azúcar añadida, keto friendly, alta proteína), and two primary CTAs: "Ver menú" and "Pedir por WhatsApp".
  3. Badges Bar: Sin gluten · Sin azúcar · Keto friendly · Alta proteína · Sin conservadores (icons + copy).
  4. Product Menu Grid: Responsive card grid with category filter tabs (Todos, Panes, Galletas, Pasteles, Postres, Bebidas). Realistic product placeholders (e.g. Pan de caja keto, Galletas de almendra cocoa/vainilla, Cheesecake de frutos rojos, Pastel tres leches saludable, Brownies sin azúcar, Smoothie proteico) with photos, price in MXN, diet badges, and a "Pedir" button.
  5. "¿Por qué DeliPanese?": 4 value blocks with clean icons and explanatory text (Ingredientes reales, Apto para diabéticos y celíacos, Horneado diario, Recetas propias artesanales).
  6. "Cómo pedir": 3 clear visual steps (1. Elige tus productos -> 2. Escríbenos por WhatsApp -> 3. Recibe en casa o recoge en tienda). Mention Colima local delivery, national shipping, and Uber Eats availability.
  7. Testimonios: Carousel/grid of 3 customer reviews with photos, names, and star ratings.
  8. Ubicación y Horarios: Embedded map placeholder (Colima, México), address details, business hours (Lunes a Viernes 8:00 am - 8:00 pm, Sábados 9:00 am - 9:00 pm), and Uber Eats direct link.
  9. Final CTA banner: "¿Se te antojó? Pide hoy mismo" with warm background and WhatsApp button.
  10. Footer: Logo, quick links, social media (@delipanese on Instagram, Facebook, TikTok), and privacy notice link.
- Functionality:
  - Floating WhatsApp action button fixed in the bottom-right corner.
  - All "Pedir" buttons open WhatsApp (`https://wa.me/521XXXXXXXXXX`) with an encoded pre-filled message mentioning the specific product.
  - Simple, elegant contact form (Nombre, Teléfono, Mensaje).
  - Mobile-first responsiveness.
  - Local SEO: Optimized document title, meta tags for "repostería sin gluten Colima", "pan keto Colima", "postres sin azúcar Colima", and JSON-LD Schema.org of type Bakery.
  - Clear, organized data structure for menu items and texts so the user can easily update photos, prices, and copy.
